Nova Gulf is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers, employees, and website visitors. Everything on this page describes exactly what our online request form offers — nothing more, nothing less.

Submit a Data Subject Request How it works

How it works

Three steps. You choose one request type per submission — if you need two different things, send two requests.

Submit your request

Pick the one right you want to exercise, tell us who you are, and send the form. You do not need an account with us to use it.

We verify your identity

We confirm the request is genuinely yours before we act on it — normally by verifying the email address you gave us.

We respond

You get a request reference, then our answer: what we did, or why we could not, and who to contact if you disagree.

What you can request

These are the six request types the Nova Gulf request form accepts. Choose one per submission — if you need two different things, send two requests.

🗑️

Erasure

Ask us to delete your personal data where the law allows.

🛠️

Rectification

Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete. The form asks which detail to fix, its current value, and what to change it to.

⏸️

Restrict Processing

Ask us to pause processing your data while a dispute is resolved.

✋

Objection

Object to processing of your personal data, including direct marketing.

📦

Port

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format, or have it sent onward.

🚫

Do not Sell or Share

Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data.

Who can submit

The form asks how you relate to Nova Gulf so it reaches the right team.

👔

Employee

Current or former Nova Gulf staff.

🧾

Customer

Anyone holding a product, account, or service with us.

👤

Other

Website visitors, applicants, suppliers, and everyone else.

Verification, timelines, and cost

📧

Email verification

We verify the email address on the request before we act on it, so nobody can request your data in your name.

⏱️

Response time

We acknowledge every request and respond within the period the applicable privacy law allows.

💷

No charge

Submitting a request is free. We will tell you in advance in the rare case a fee is lawful.

A cookie notice you will see on the form. The request form sets one strictly necessary cookie to prevent cross-site request forgery. It collects no personal information about you.
