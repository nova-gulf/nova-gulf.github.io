What we use cookies for

Every cookie and tracker this site sets is placed in one of these categories. Only the first one is always on; you can turn the rest off.

🔐

Essential

Needed for the site to work at all — keeping you signed in, remembering the cookie choice you just made, and protecting the site from abuse. These cannot be switched off.

📈

Analytics & Customization

Tells us which pages get read and where people give up. We use it to fix the site, not to identify you.

⚙️

Performance & Functionality

Remembers preferences such as your language or region so you do not set them again on every visit.

📣

Advertising

Used to decide which of our messages you see elsewhere on the web. Off unless you turn it on.

💬

Social Networking

Set by social platforms when a page embeds their content — a video, a share button, a feed.

❔

Unclassified

Something our scan found that we have not finished categorising yet. We show it rather than hide it.

What happens when you opt out

The script never runs

We do not delete a cookie after it is set — we stop the script that would have set it from loading in the first place. That is a stronger guarantee than deletion.

Cookies already on your device stay

Anything set before you opted out remains in your browser until it expires or you clear it. Nothing new is sent to the third party, because the script that would send it no longer runs.

Your choice is recorded

We keep a dated record of what you chose, so we can show a regulator what you were asked and what you answered.

Browser signals we honour

You do not have to use our banner to tell us what you want.

🛰️

Global Privacy Control (GPC)

If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of the categories mapped to it and tell you we detected it, rather than asking you again.

🧾

A formal request

If you would rather put it in writing, the request form accepts a Do Not Sell or Share request.

If the button does nothing

The Manage Cookie Preferences button is provided by the consent SDK. If clicking it has no effect, the SDK has not loaded — these are the three usual reasons.

A blocker is stopping it

Tracker-blocking extensions frequently block consent SDKs as well. Try a private window with extensions disabled.

The configuration has not published yet

A newly deployed banner can take time to reach the content delivery layer. Reload after a few minutes.

You already answered, and nothing changed

The banner does not reappear once you have answered. That is by design — this page is how you get back to it.
